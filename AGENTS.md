# Tiger Strike visual quality

- All new and revised artwork must match or exceed the detailed graphics currently used in Tiger Strike missions.
- Use the production shared soldier, tiger, civilian, scenery, water and vehicle renderers in Solo, Co-op, previews, practice areas and cinematics. Do not substitute simplified showroom-only shapes or downgraded character models.
- Preview the actual purchased equipment and livery on the same models used in gameplay. Match production terrain detail, lighting, shadows and animation; keep controls, objectives and labels readable on phones.
- Vehicle upgrades must propagate to gameplay and preview/cinematic consumers together. Keep boarding, departure paths, collisions, mission state and paid ownership rules intact.
- Verify representative desktop and phone-width scenes visually, plus applicable behavior tests. State any unverified physical Telegram checks accurately.
