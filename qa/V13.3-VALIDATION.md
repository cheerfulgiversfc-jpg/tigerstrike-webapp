# V13.3 validation

## Reproduce locally

Use a recent Node runtime, with no production KV environment configured:

```
node --test --test-concurrency=4 *.test.js api/_lib/squad-session.test.js
node qa/benchmark-combat.js
node qa/performance-lab-server.js
```

The optional browser lab listens only at http://127.0.0.1:8774/. Both test
clients and their fake identities use memory-only services. The lab refuses
configured persistent storage. Its extra crowd and river are synthetic render
load, not a claim of completing a real campaign mission or physical swim test.

## Results and limits

- 361 automated tests pass, including eight new performance/network tests.
- Two isolated actual client implementations exercise a real memory-backed
  capture, crowded river rendering, separate Shop/Inventory profiles, purchase
  isolation, dropped resume retries and connection recovery together.
- Off-camera artwork is skipped without changing authoritative actors. Visible
  bosses, cages, overlapping blood halos and rescue-door arrivals remain drawn.
- Irregular packet delivery is checked at 15, 30 and 60 simulated FPS; prediction
  remains bounded and stale/restarted snapshots do not restore old movement.
- Repeated presence packets preserve unchanged equipment buttons. Actual
  purchases still refresh balances. This was found during browser checking.
- Node command-generation benchmark: 60 tigers, 40 civilians, two soldiers,
  chapter river and cage; 60 measured frames after warm-up. Baseline: 45,646
  canvas calls/frame, about 8.5 ms median. Updated: 21,121 calls/frame, about
  5.2 ms median / 5.5 ms p95. Timings vary. This does not measure GPU work,
  phone FPS, memory pressure or Telegram performance.
- Browser lab checks use 120–1400 ms response delays and every fifth sync
  failing. Both gear panels open concurrently; a rubber-ammunition purchase
  changes only its buyer's balance; one exiting does not resume the other's
  open panel. Normal connectivity can be restored using the lab control.

## Physical acceptance still pending

These changes do not certify two physical Telegram phones on a weak network.
On those devices, check both viewpoints while moving among a crowded fight,
crossing water and capturing a tiger. Open Shop on one and Inventory on the
other; buy, equip and close each. Verify pause/resume and separate supplies.
Disconnect one phone briefly, confirm the connection warning, then reconnect
and verify the same squad, authoritative positions and unchanged rewards.
Record device models, network conditions and any remaining hitch before
calling the physical acceptance test complete.
